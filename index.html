Equipment members keep praising.

03 — Coaching

Trainers who stay on the floor with you.

Ask a Levels member what keeps them coming back and the answer is usually the trainers: supportive, competent, and there when you need a spotter or a correction. Members describe coaches with real bodybuilding experience — people who have competed and won — and a floor where beginners are shown what to do, not left to guess.

  • Form checked on the floor, not just a programme handed over
  • Goals set around weight loss or muscle gain — your call
  • A floor where regulars look out for each other
